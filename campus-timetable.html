How the recommendation is ranked

Keep the same day where possible, move one session only, avoid teacher/room/cohort clashes, respect room capacity, then prefer the smallest time change. The ranking is visible, not an opaque “AI” claim.

Applying changes currently updates this browser’s schedule only. Shared publishing and notifications must be connected to authenticated institutional services.

Designed for a real coordinator

Keep the hand-offs visible—not just the algorithm.

  • Draft before publish: keep the current timetable safe until a person reviews the proposed move.
  • Explain the trade-off: show which class and cohort are affected and why alternatives were rejected.
  • Sell a workflow: pilot with one department, then validate campus/department pricing and supported scheduling volume with coordinators.
Institutional service connections: shared schedule storage, campus/department roles, student-system enrollment and course data, room and instructor availability, audit history, calendar sync and approved notifications must connect through authenticated backend services before campus deployment.
